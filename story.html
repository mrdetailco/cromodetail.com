The story

Cromo means
chrome.

Chrome is the honest finish. It doesn't hide anything — it reflects exactly what's in front of it. If the work is flawed, chrome shows the flaw. If the work is right, chrome proves it.

That's the standard this studio is named after. Cromo is chrome in Spanish and Italian, from the Greek chroma — and it's the level every car that comes through this process is held to: a finish clean enough to check your reflection in.

Cromo Detail Co. is a founder-run mobile studio serving Orange County. One truck, fully self-contained, built so the full studio process — filtered water, power, steam, compressed air, extraction — arrives at your driveway. No queue, no rushed turnaround, no handing your keys to whoever's on shift. The person who answers your text is the person detailing your car.

The mark

Precision.
Protection.
Presence.

Three words on every card, and a promise in order: the work is exact, the surfaces leave protected, and the result is a car you notice from across the lot. That's the whole brand. Everything else is just keeping it true, one driveway at a time.
