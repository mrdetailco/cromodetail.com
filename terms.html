Who we are

Cromo Detail Co. is a mobile auto-detailing service operating in Orange County, California. These terms cover every booking made by text, by phone, through cromodetail.com, or in person. By booking a service, you agree to them.

Booking and confirmation

A booking is confirmed when we reply to confirm the date, time, service, and price. Until then, nothing is reserved. We book by text so there's always a written record of what was agreed.

Pricing

Services are priced by tier and vehicle size, and the prices on cromodetail.com are the prices you pay. Two exceptions: add-ons listed as "from" a price, and vehicles in heavy condition (pet hair, sand, spills, mold, smoke, or anything biohazardous), are confirmed from a photo or on arrival before any work begins. You'll always hear the number before we start. If it's not for you, you can decline on the spot at no charge.

What we need on site

A safe, reasonably level, legal place to park next to the vehicle, such as your driveway or a private lot with permission, with room to open every door. We bring our own water and power, so you don't need to supply anything. Please clear personal items and valuables out of the car beforehand. We set aside anything we find, but we can't be responsible for cash, documents, or valuables left inside.

Rescheduling and cancellations

Plans change, and that's fine. Text us as early as you can. Cancelling or moving an appointment with at least 24 hours' notice costs nothing. For same-day cancellations, or if nobody is available when we arrive, we may ask for a deposit before your next booking.

Weather

A detail needs conditions where the finish can be done right. If rain, high wind, or extreme heat is in the forecast, we'll reschedule together at no penalty.

Your vehicle's existing condition

We look the vehicle over before starting and will point out existing damage we see: scratches, rock chips, failing clear coat, loose or brittle trim, set-in stains, odors. Detailing cleans and protects; it does not repair prior damage, and some stains, etching, and odors may not come out fully. When we think something won't, we'll tell you up front rather than after.

If something goes wrong

We use surface-correct, coating-safe products and work carefully on every car. If you believe something was damaged by our work, text us within 48 hours with photos and we'll make it right. The same goes for anything missed: tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back and fix it at no charge. We aren't responsible for problems caused by pre-existing wear, prior repairs, aftermarket parts, or trim that was already loose or failing.

Payment

Payment is due at handoff, once you've looked the car over. We take cards on site.

Photos

We photograph vehicles before and after service, both for our records and to show our work. License plates are blurred, and we never post your name, address, or anything else that identifies you. If you'd prefer your car not appear in our work, say so when booking and it won't.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version on cromodetail.com is the current one and applies to bookings made after it's posted.
