Privacy policy

Your car,
your information.

What we collect when you book, what we do with it, and what we never do.

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

When you book, we collect what we need to show up and do the job: your name, phone number, the address where the vehicle will be, details about the vehicle, and the messages you send us. If you email us, we have your email address. We also photograph vehicles before and after service. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we don't see or store your full card number.

How we use it

To schedule and perform your detail, to reply to you, to keep a record of past jobs so repeat visits are easier, and to take payment. We don't send marketing messages you didn't ask for.

Photos of your vehicle

Before-and-after photos may appear on cromodetail.com and on our social media accounts. License plates are blurred, and we don't post your name, address, or anything else that identifies you. If you'd rather your vehicle not be shown, text us at any time and it won't be, or will be taken down.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information to anyone. It's shared only with the services we use to run the business: our card payment processor, the phone and email services we communicate through, and our website host. Each sees only what it needs to do its part.

This website

cromodetail.com is a simple static site. It has no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics tracking. The booking page runs entirely in your browser and only sends information when you tap to open a text message. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address when they're fetched.

How long we keep it

Job records (your name, vehicle, service, date, and price) are kept for as long as you're a customer and as long as needed for basic bookkeeping. Photos are kept as part of our portfolio unless you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

Text or email us at any time to see what we have, correct it, delete it, or opt out of photos. We'll handle it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
