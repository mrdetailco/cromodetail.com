Process

The sequence
never changes.

Order is the difference between washing a car and detailing one. Chemistry first, pressure second, protection always last.

Pre-soak

Bugs, tar, and grime get chemistry and dwell time before anything touches the paint.

Koch-Chemie Green Star

Snow foam

A thick foam blanket lifts loose dirt off the surface so the wash stays scratch-free.

Koch-Chemie Gentle Snow Foam

Contact wash

Two-bucket hand wash with coating-safe shampoo — safe on ceramic, sealants, and bare clear coat alike.

CarPro Reset · P&S Pearl

Decontaminate

Tar removal and clay treatment pull out what washing can't, leaving paint glass-smooth. (Full & Premium)

CarPro TarX · Gyeon Clay

Interior

Compressed-air deep clean, steam where it's needed, and surface-correct products for leather, plastics, and fabric.

Tornador · Dupray steam · Koch-Chemie Pol Star

Protect

Paint sealed, trim and tires dressed, glass finished. The shine is the receipt — protection is the product.

Bead Maker · CarPro DLUX · DarkSide
