FAQ

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Where do you work?

Orange County, California — we come to your home or workplace anywhere in the county. Text your location when booking and we'll confirm.

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. The rig is fully self-contained — onboard filtered water and onboard power run everything, including the pressure washer, steam, and extraction. All we need is space next to the vehicle.

How do I book?

Text or call (949) 285-4662 with your vehicle, the tier you're thinking about, and your area. You'll get a real reply from the person doing the work — usually same day.

Which tier should I pick?

A simple rule: if the car is washed regularly and just needs upkeep, Maintenance. If it's been a few months — or it's never had a proper deep clean — Full Detail. If you want it as close to new as detailing can take it, Premium.

Not sure? Text a photo. You'll get an honest recommendation, not the most expensive one.

Is it safe for ceramic-coated or freshly painted cars?

Yes — the wash chemistry is coating-safe by design, and the process adapts to what your paint has on it. If your car has fresh paint or wrap, mention it when booking so the right products are staged.

What about heavy messes — pet hair, sand, spills?

All handleable. Heavy conditions just take longer, so they may be quoted individually before work begins. Send a photo with your booking text and you'll get a firm price up front — no surprises on arrival.

What if it rains?

We'll reschedule together, no penalty. A detail deserves conditions where the finish can actually be done right.

Do you offer regular maintenance plans?

Yes — many clients set a recurring Maintenance schedule after a Full or Premium detail, which is the best way to keep the results year-round. Ask about it at your first appointment.
